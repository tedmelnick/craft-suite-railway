Drive these apps from your own Claude

Works with every app except Craft Anything. This site’s own Claude connection is private, so you run a small relay of your own — a tiny server that links Claude to the app open in your browser.

  • Optional. Every app works fully on its own; Claude is an extra.
  • Ask in plain words — “make a budget table”, “fill in this PDF form” — and Claude works in the app open in your browser.
  • Your own relay, your own costs. About ten minutes to set up once, on your own Claude and Railway accounts.
  • You stay in control. Nothing connects until you press Connect; Claude only reaches that tab, with a read-only switch and Disconnect in the ⦾ panel.
